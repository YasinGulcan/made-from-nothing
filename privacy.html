Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how the Made From Nothing publishing tool ("the app") handles data. The app is a private, internal tool operated by the owner of the Made From Nothing channels. It is used only to publish our own videos to our own accounts and to read statistics about those videos.

Who uses the app

The only user of the app is the channel owner. The app does not offer sign-in to the public and does not access, collect or process data belonging to other people's accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner's permission, the app uploads videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload), reads the owner's channel and video information (youtube.readonly), and reads view and engagement statistics for the owner's own videos (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Instagram (Meta): the app publishes Reels to the owner's Instagram professional account and reads insights for those posts.
  • TikTok: the app uploads videos to the owner's TikTok account and reads basic account and video information.

How the data is used

Access tokens and statistics are used only to publish the owner's videos and to measure how those videos perform, so that future videos can be improved. Data is not used for advertising and is not used to build profiles of any person.

Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to third-party servers, except when calling the official YouTube, Instagram or TikTok APIs to carry out the actions described above.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through these APIs with anyone.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, Instagram/Facebook "Apps and Websites" settings, or TikTok "Manage app permissions". To request deletion of any stored data, email us; we delete it within 30 days.
